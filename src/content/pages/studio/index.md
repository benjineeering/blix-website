## Cancellation

The customer may cancel the rental agreement free of charge up to 7 days before the start of the rental period. In case of a later cancellation, a cancellation fee of 50% of the rental price will be charged.

In the event of a no-show or a last-minute cancellation within 24 hours, the full rental price will be charged.
