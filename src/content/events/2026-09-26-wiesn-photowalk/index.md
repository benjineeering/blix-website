📸🍻 The Wiesn Photo Walk Bingo continues September 26th!

On our Oktoberfest photo walk, capture classic Wiesn moments and tick them off the bingo card. Aim for a line: vertical, horizontal, or diagonal! 🎡🥨🍺

Upload your finished bingo on Instagram with the hashtag [#blixmscwiesn](https://www.instagram.com/explore/tags/blixmscwiesn/), and follow and tag [@blix_munich](https://www.instagram.com/blix_munich/) & [@munichstreetcollective](https://www.instagram.com/munichstreetcollective/).

**Meeting point:** blix, 2 PM. More info on our Instagram [@blix_munich](https://www.instagram.com/blix_munich/).

- 🗓️ **Deadline:** End of Oktoberfest (hopefully all analog photos will be developed by then…)
- 🏆 **Prize:** a fabulous gift basket, sponsored by blix and MSC 🎁🎞️
